About These Public Copies

The notebooks preserve the research code and written explanations. Saved execution outputs, including embedded clinical images, have been removed from these downloadable copies.

Some notebooks require external dataset files, trained model checkpoints, or changes to Google Drive paths. They are provided as research archives and may require configuration before they can be executed again.

DATASET INFORMATION

Fitzpatrick17k Dataset

Our research uses the Fitzpatrick17k dataset, which includes skin-condition images and associated annotations.

The original dataset repository is publicly available on GitHub. The image data used during this study was obtained separately from the dataset's maintainers under specific research-use conditions.

We do not redistribute the clinical image dataset. Researchers seeking access should consult the original maintainers and follow the applicable conditions.

Visit Original Dataset Repository ↗

Research note: In this study, the “Fair” domain refers to Fitzpatrick types I–III, while the “Dark” domain refers to types IV–VI. These are experimental grouping labels; the term “Fair” does not mean that a model has been demonstrated to be fair. The research findings should not be interpreted as clinical validation.
