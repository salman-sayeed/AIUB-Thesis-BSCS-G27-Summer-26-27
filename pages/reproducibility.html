How to Explore or Reproduce the Work

These steps provide a starting point. Some research files and configuration details must be supplied separately.

  1. 01

    Obtain the Research Notebooks

    Download the public Jupyter notebooks from our research archive. The notebooks document eight stages of the study, beginning with dataset exploration and ending with exploratory visual explanations.

    Explore Research Notebooks ↗
  2. 02

    Prepare the Computing Environment

    Open the notebooks in a compatible Python/Jupyter environment. Google Colab was used for the original research, with an NVIDIA T4 GPU available during the experiments.

    Review the notebook imports and setup cells to identify the required software libraries. Dependency versions may differ from those in the original runs.

    A GPU can reduce training time, but obtaining a T4 or equivalent accelerator is not guaranteed on the Colab free tier.
  3. 03

    Obtain Authorized Dataset Access

    The study uses the Fitzpatrick17k dataset. Its original GitHub repository provides information about the dataset and publicly available annotation resources.

    The clinical image files were obtained separately from the dataset's maintainers under individual research-use conditions. They are not included in our public notebook downloads.

    Fitzpatrick17k Original Repository ↗
    Anyone attempting image-based experiments must obtain their own authorized access and follow the dataset owner's conditions.
  4. 04

    Review File Paths and Required Artifacts

    The notebooks contain references to the original research environment. Before execution, review paths for dataset files, Google Drive folders, saved model checkpoints, and intermediate analysis files.

    These resources may need to be recreated or configured for another environment.

  5. 05

    Follow the Experimental Phases

    Review the notebooks in their numbered research order: initial analysis, baseline classification, domain generalization, controlled training composition, mechanistic analysis, domain adaptation, broader group evaluation, and Grad-CAM++ assessment.

    Check the inputs and outputs required by each notebook before running it. The notebooks are not a fully automated end-to-end pipeline.

  6. 06

    Compare Results With the Published Study

    After completing the applicable experiments, compare the generated results against the tables and figures provided on this website.

    Differences may occur because of software versions, computing resources, random variation, dataset preparation, or unavailable intermediate artifacts.

    View Tables ↗ View Figures ↗

Important Limitations

Transparency about what these materials can and cannot support.

  • Not a one-click reproduction package. The notebooks require external data, path configuration, and potentially additional research artifacts.
  • Exact numerical reproduction is not guaranteed. Differences in software environments, computational resources, and experimental execution may affect results.
  • Dataset usage is subject to permission. The research team does not grant access to or redistribution rights for the original clinical images.
  • Public notebooks omit saved outputs. The downloadable copies are intended to share research code and explanations, without distributing the original execution history or clinical image data.
  • This is an experimental research study. The findings do not establish clinical validity, deployment readiness, or fairness across all populations.
